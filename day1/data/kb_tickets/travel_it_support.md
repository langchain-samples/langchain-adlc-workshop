# IT Support While Traveling

## Before you travel

### Checklist
- [ ] Laptop is fully charged and has the latest security patches
- [ ] VPN client is working (test from home first)
- [ ] MFA is set up and working on your phone
- [ ] Important files are synced to OneDrive/SharePoint (not just local)
- [ ] You have the IT helpdesk contact number saved in your phone

### International travel
- Notify IT before traveling internationally — some countries have restrictions on encrypted devices
- Check if your destination requires a special VPN configuration
- Be aware of local data protection laws

## During travel

### Connectivity
- **Hotel Wi-Fi:** Often blocks VPN — try switching VPN protocol from SSL to IPSec
- **Mobile hotspot:** Use your phone's hotspot as a backup connection
- **Public Wi-Fi:** Always use VPN; never access sensitive data without it

### Device security
- Keep your laptop with you at all times — never check it in luggage
- Use a privacy screen in public places
- Lock your device whenever you step away

### Time zone issues
- Outlook calendar should auto-adjust to the local time zone
- If meetings appear at wrong times, check Outlook's time zone setting

## Emergency contacts
- **IT Helpdesk:** Available 07:00–19:00 CET, Monday–Friday
- **After-hours emergency:** On-call engineer for P1 issues (security incidents, complete system outage)
- **Lost/stolen device:** Report immediately to Security and IT
