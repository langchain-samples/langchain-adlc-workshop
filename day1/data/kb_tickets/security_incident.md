# How to Report a Security Incident

## What counts as a security incident

- Phishing emails with suspicious links or attachments
- Unauthorized access to systems or data
- Lost or stolen devices containing work data
- Malware or ransomware detection
- Data leakage (accidental or intentional)
- Suspicious network activity

## How to report

### Immediate threats (active attack, data breach)
1. Call the Security Operations Center (SOC) immediately
2. Do not attempt to remediate on your own
3. Preserve evidence — don't delete emails, files, or logs

### Non-immediate concerns (suspicious email, potential vulnerability)
1. Forward phishing emails to the security team via the "Report Phishing" button
2. Submit a security incident report via the IT portal
3. Include as much detail as possible: what happened, when, what systems were affected

## What happens next
1. SOC triages the report and assesses severity
2. If confirmed, an incident response team is assigned
3. You may be contacted for additional information
4. The incident is documented and tracked to resolution

## Important notes
- **Never** open suspicious attachments or click suspicious links
- **Never** share credentials via email or phone
- Reporting a potential incident is always better than ignoring it
- You will not be penalized for reporting a security concern in good faith
