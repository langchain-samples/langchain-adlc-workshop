# What to Do When Locked Out of Your Account

## Common causes
- Too many failed login attempts (account lockout policy)
- Password expired without notice
- Account disabled due to inactivity or security policy
- MFA device lost or not working

## Resolution steps

### Self-service (preferred)
1. Go to the self-service password reset portal
2. Enter your employee ID and answer security questions
3. Reset your password and unlock your account

### Helpdesk-assisted
1. Call or visit the IT helpdesk with your employee badge
2. Verify identity (badge number, manager confirmation, or security questions)
3. Helpdesk will unlock the account and/or reset the password

### MFA-related lockout
- If MFA is the issue, see `reset_mfa.md` for the MFA reset procedure
- MFA reset requires identity verification before proceeding

## Prevention
- Set up self-service password reset before you need it
- Keep recovery email and phone number up to date
- Don't wait until the last day to change an expiring password
