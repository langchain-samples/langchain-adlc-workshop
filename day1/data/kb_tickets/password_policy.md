# Password Requirements and Reset Process

## Password requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Minimum length | 14 characters |
| Complexity | Must include uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character |
| History | Cannot reuse the last 12 passwords |
| Expiration | Every 90 days |
| Lockout | 5 failed attempts → 30-minute lockout |

## How to change your password

### Before expiration
1. Press Ctrl+Alt+Delete → "Change a password" (Windows)
2. Or go to System Settings → Users & Groups → Change Password (Mac)

### After expiration
1. You will be prompted to change your password at login
2. Follow the on-screen instructions

### Self-service reset (forgot password)
1. Go to the self-service password reset portal
2. Verify identity with security questions or MFA
3. Create a new password

## Tips for strong passwords
- Use a passphrase: "Coffee-Morning-Sunshine-42!" is stronger and easier to remember than "Xk#9mP2$"
- Use a password manager for unique passwords across systems
- Never write passwords on sticky notes or share them

## Important notes
- IT will **never** ask for your password via email or phone
- Change your password immediately if you suspect it has been compromised
- Enable MFA on all accounts that support it
