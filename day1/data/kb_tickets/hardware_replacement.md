# Hardware Replacement Policy and Process

## When hardware is replaced

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| Device is defective (under warranty) | Warranty repair or replacement |
| Device is defective (out of warranty, < 4 years) | Repair if cost-effective, otherwise replace |
| Device is 4+ years old | Scheduled replacement |
| Device is damaged (accidental) | Report to IT; replacement subject to manager approval |

## How to request a replacement

1. **Report the issue** — Submit a ticket describing the hardware problem
2. **Diagnosis** — IT will diagnose the issue remotely or in person
3. **Approval** — Manager approval required for replacement
4. **Loaner** — A loaner device is provided while the replacement is prepared
5. **Data transfer** — IT will migrate your data to the new device
6. **Return** — Return the old device to IT for secure disposal

## Timeline
- Diagnosis: 1-2 business days
- Replacement (in-stock): 3-5 business days
- Replacement (order needed): 2-4 weeks

## Important notes
- Always back up your data before bringing in a device
- Remove personal files and applications before returning old hardware
- All returned devices are securely wiped per Acme data handling policy
